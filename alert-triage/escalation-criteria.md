# Escalation Criteria

## Escalate to Senior Analyst
- Confirmed malware execution on endpoint
- Lateral movement detected
- Multiple systems affected
- Phishing with confirmed credential entry
- Anomalous admin account activity

## Escalate to IR Team
- Active ransomware spreading
- Data exfiltration in progress
- Domain admin account compromised
- Business-critical system down due to attack

## Escalate to Management
- Breach affecting customer data
- Regulatory reporting required (GDPR, HIPAA)
- Attack by nation-state level threat actor
- Media/PR risk involved

## Never Escalate Without
- Timeline of events
- Affected assets list
- IOCs identified
- Actions already taken
- Current status of threat
